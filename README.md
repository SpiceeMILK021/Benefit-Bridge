## 💎 The Main Event: Unified Application Drafting
**Stop filling out the same forms over and over.** The standout feature of Benefit Bridge is the **Application Profile System**. 
By entering your household data once, the app constructs a comprehensive digital draft. This draft acts as a "Master Application" that can be used to instantly screen for multiple subsidies without re-entering a single piece of information.

## 🚀 Core Features

### 📋 Full-Service Document Preparation
* **One-Click Drafting**: Automatically compile household size, income details, and program interests into a structured profile.
* **Local Persistence**: The app saves your progress to a local `benefit_bridge_draft.json` file. Stop today, finish tomorrow—your data never leaves your computer.
* **Professional Exports**: Export your full session results to JSON for easy sharing with social workers or for your own records.

### 🔍 Intelligence-Driven Screening
* **Multi-Program Evaluation**: Instantly check eligibility for **Child Care, Food Assistance, Utility Relief, Internet Subsidies, and Transportation Vouchers**.
* **Dynamic "What-If" Modeling**: Use the real-time income slider to see exactly how changes in your monthly earnings affect your benefit eligibility.
* **State-Specific Logic**: Includes built-in income thresholds for dozens of states (AL, AK, AZ, AR, CA, CO, CT, DC, and more), ensuring calculations are localized and accurate.

### 📍 Intelligent Office Radar
* **Geographic Precision**: Finds the nearest service offices using ZIP code and City coordinate mapping.
* **Integrated Logistics**: View distances and jump directly to Google Maps for navigation with a single click.
