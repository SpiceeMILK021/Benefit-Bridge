Benefit Bridge
Benefit Bridge is a privacy-first, desktop-based eligibility estimator and office locator designed to help individuals discover support programs like child care, food assistance, utility help, and more.

🚀 Key Features
Eligibility Screening: Instant estimation for five major assistance areas:

Child-care subsidies: Licensed care support.

Food assistance: Monthly grocery support (SNAP-like).

Utility bill help: Energy, water, or emergency bill support.

Internet subsidies: Low-cost digital access.

Transportation vouchers: Transit passes for work or medical needs.

State-Specific Rules: Includes built-in income limits and eligibility thresholds for dozens of U.S. states (AL, AK, AZ, AR, CA, CO, CT, DC, etc.).

Office Locator: Search for nearby service offices by ZIP code or city with a customizable search radius.

Privacy-First Persistence:

Local Drafts: Automatically saves progress to benefit_bridge_draft.json for later retrieval.

Secure Exports: Export full session data to JSON for archiving or external review.

Modern Accessibility: Dark-themed UI with adjustable font scaling, "Reduce Motion" settings, and keyboard shortcuts.
